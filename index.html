Hearing aids, from sound wave to signal

A hearing aid does not just make sound louder. It listens, shapes and compresses sound to fit one person’s hearing loss.

Without AGC a loud sound is clipped and distorts. With AGC the circuit compresses the loud part so it stays comfortable.

Audiogram lab: fitting an aid to a loss

Most wearers lose high frequencies first. Move the slider to change the severity of loss, and see how a fitted aid lifts the thresholds.

average loss (500 Hz to 4 kHz)

The active filters attenuate low frequencies by up to 30 to 40 dB relative to high frequencies, because wearers mainly need high-frequency gain.

Cochlear implant: electrodes replace hair cells

For sensori-neural deafness, the implant splits sound into frequency bands and sends each band to an electrode in the scala tympani. Pick a pitch and see which electrode fires.
